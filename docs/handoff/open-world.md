## What is already built

Do not rebuild these.

- **The city** is one persistent co-op room per district (`server/city-room.ts`). `pvp: false`, `ai: true`. Another player's bullets do not land (`shared/sim/world.ts`, the `!this.pvp` return). A file that walks in gets eight seconds of street shield. Five districts, gates as doors, a world map, a presence feed (`GET /city`).
- **Things to do today:** public events HOLD / INTERCEPT / ESCORT (`shared/city/events.ts`) and three street-run courses per district (`shared/city/courses.ts`). Both pay file XP and stamps only (`shared/city/reward.ts`). A day of events is capped under one good match. A course pays once in a file's life. That cap is the point.
- **The campaign** is a contract list with a crew code, settled on the campaign host. PLAY already walks into the busiest district. The contracts are not standing in the street.
- **THE RUN** (Stage 14) is already the PvP extraction mode: claims on the ground, a safe gate, a Depth-10 gate, 200 units a day, a nightly pot (`shared/economy/settlement.ts`) paid through the PrizeVault. Below Depth 10 it pays Scrip, not $CAPITAL. Ordinary matches never pay $CAPITAL.
- **Three ledgers.** Scrip buys progression breadth and is not transferable. Wakelight buys cosmetics and is not transferable. $CAPITAL is the only transferable token, and it buys identity, cosmetics, room-hours, and competition. It never buys a stat. `npm run lint:economy` and `npm run lint:progression` fail the build when a priced item grows a mechanical field.
- **Room Credits** (`RoomCredits.sol`) are on-chain hours for a private server, 100% burned. They are not the credit this assignment means. Do not overload that contract.
- **The phone.** The combat row is the weapon slots plus one DESK chip (`client/hud/hud.ts`, commit `d6699d0`). FILE, GRAPH, MAP, MARKET, CONTRACTS, and NAME open from that sheet. Do not put the six tabs back on the combat row.

## The four jobs

Ship them in this order. Each one is done when its test fails if the new line is removed, and when the lints named at the bottom are still clean.

### 1. A credit that is not Scrip and not a room-hour

Add one off-chain balance on the Ghostfile. Call it a **chit** in code and on the ledger line, so it cannot be confused with Room Credits or with Scrip.

Rules the tests have to hold:

- A public city event, a street run, and a campaign contract pay **zero chits**. They keep paying XP and stamps exactly as they do now. A PvE activity that pays a cash-out is a faucet, and `shared/city/reward.ts` exists to forbid it.
- Chits are earned only where another player can take them: inside a contest volume (job 2) and, if you fold the numbers together, as THE RUN's existing carried units. One balance, not two that both become $CAPITAL.
- Chits do not transfer by trade, gift, or mail. The only way a chit leaves a file for another file is by being dropped in a contest when the carrier dies, the way a RUN claim already drops.
- Spending a chit never writes damage, health, shield, recoil, spread, a node, a chip, a weapon, or a contract flag. A test that puts a chit price on any of those fails `lint:economy` and `lint:progression`.
- Depth below 10 can hold chits and can lose them. It cannot exchange them. That is the same gate THE RUN already uses.

### 2. PvP as a block in the city, not a flag on the city

Do not set the city room to `pvp: true`. That removes the street shield and lets every stranger shoot the spawn.

Add one contest volume per district, marked on the ground and on the world map, using geometry that is already a street. Inside the volume, player damage applies. Outside it, the existing PvE rule applies, including the gates. Walking out drops nothing; dying inside drops the chits you carried in, at your feet, for anyone, and they expire back to the district if nobody takes them. Respawn at the nearest gate. Same guns, same health. No loadout you can buy.

The contest pays from a **fixed pot for the session**, split by placement, or from the chits the dead dropped. It does not mint a chit per kill. A kill with an empty pocket pays the killer nothing.

Kernel Protocols stay out of any room where players can hurt each other. `tests/quarantine.test.ts` already fails if the PvP match imports `shared/campaign` power, `shared/economy`, or `server/chain`. Keep that. City code stays in `shared/city/`. The match worker does not import it.

Safe zones stay safe: the gates, and the market booth Stage 714 already uses to enter THE RUN. Standing in a gate clears the carried chits into the file's balance only after the same two-second dwell THE RUN uses. That dwell is the exchange into the file, not yet into $CAPITAL.

### 3. The campaign standing in the street

The contract list stays the source of missions. A fixer stands at an existing lot (do not add a box, a sign, a fence, or a light on Lease Row; that district's city frame is 189,998 triangles). The interact key offers the contract the file has already reached. Completing it in the street settles the same way the crew room does: XP, stamps, the file's arc. Zero chits.

Two more things to do, both server-timed, both using systems that exist:

- A **carry** between two gates of the same district. The clock is the street-run clock. First completion of a route stamps once. Later completions count and pay nothing.
- The public events that already start on a timer also start when a file accepts one at the fixer, still capped at six paying events a day.

The world map lists who is in the district and whether a contest or an event is up. It already has the presence feed. Extend that feed. Do not publish a file id; display names only.

One verb. Hold interact on a fixer, a claim, or a gate. If `Btn` / `ACTION_MASK` has to grow, update the protocol fingerprint test in the same change. Do not add a second row of buttons on the phone.

### 4. Exchanging chits for $CAPITAL, and $CAPITAL for chits

Two directions, two machines. Neither one buys a stat.

**Chits to $CAPITAL.** The file's banked chits are units. They settle through `shared/economy/settlement.ts` and a PrizeVault epoch, the same night job THE RUN uses. The day's pot is unchanged. Adding chits to the day's units makes each unit worth less, which is the point of the pot. Do not add a per-chit price. Do not pay at the ceiling before the day ends, except on the devnet path that already refuses a settled day. Below Depth 10 the exchange pays Scrip, as THE RUN does, and the Scrip still cannot be sold for $CAPITAL.

**$CAPITAL to chits.** This direction is a sink. Burning $CAPITAL (the Season Buyout / Room Credits pattern: the buyer names the price, 100% burned) credits chits. Those chits can be carried into a contest or spent on what $CAPITAL already buys (a cosmetic, a room-hour, a name). They cannot be spent on progression. There is no yield, no stake that pays a return, no odds, and no house side of a bet. A fixed prize pool for a contest is competition. A quoted payout on a kill is not.

The desk is a panel the file already knows how to open (MARKET or FILE), plus one ledger line. On the phone it is a button on the DESK sheet, not a new combat widget. The live counter stays unconfigured. If chain id, RPC, and a capital address are absent, the exchange says so and does not invent a balance.

## What "finished" means here

A stranger can play for ten minutes and do four things without a menu dive: walk in with other people, take a job off a person in the street, step into a marked block and lose a fight, and see the chits they banked named as units that settle tonight. The guns did not change. The phone still has one DESK chip. Lease Row's frame is still 189,998.
